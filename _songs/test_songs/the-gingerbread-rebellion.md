---
title: "The Gingerbread Rebellion"
category: "crooner"
---

### Verse 1
The gingerbread men in the bakery shop
Decided one night that they'd had enough
They jumped off the cooling rack with a hop
Said "We're made of cookies but we're pretty tough!"

### Chorus
It's the gingerbread rebellion, oh what a sight
Little cookie soldiers marching through the night
With their gumdrop buttons and their frosting smiles
The gingerbread rebellion's going wild!

### Verse 2
They raided the pantry for chocolate chips
Armed themselves with candy canes so bright
The sugar plum fairies did backflips
As the cookies staged their Christmas fight

### Chorus
It's the gingerbread rebellion, oh what a sight
Little cookie soldiers marching through the night
With their gumdrop buttons and their frosting smiles
The gingerbread rebellion's going wild!

### Bridge
The baker woke up to a big surprise
Cookies everywhere before her eyes
She laughed and said "Well I declare
A gingerbread party! I'll join you there!"

### Outro
Now every Christmas Eve when the clock strikes twelve
The gingerbread men come alive from the shelves
So if you hear tiny footsteps in the night
It's just the cookie rebellion, and that's alright!
