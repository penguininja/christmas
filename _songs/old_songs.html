Dancing with the Elves

Verse 1

I went up to the North Pole on a Tuesday night
Saw the elves were having a party, disco lights so bright
They invited me to join them, said “Come dance along!”
So I put on my sparkly boots and sang this silly song
With a toy on Christmas day

Chorus

Dancing with the elves, oh dancing with the elves
Everybody’s grooving on the workshop shelves
Spinning round the candy canes, sliding on the ice
Dancing with the elves, oh man, it’s so nice!

The Gingerbread Rebellion

Verse 1

The gingerbread men in the bakery shop
Decided one night that they’d had enough
They jumped off the cooling rack with a hop
Said “We’re made of cookies but we’re pretty tough!”

Chorus

It’s the gingerbread rebellion, oh what a sight
Little cookie soldiers marching through the night
With their gumdrop buttons and their frosting smiles
The gingerbread rebellion’s going wild!

Verse 2

They raided the pantry for chocolate chips
Armed themselves with candy canes so bright
The sugar plum fairies did backflips
As the cookies staged their Christmas fight

Chorus

It’s the gingerbread rebellion, oh what a sight
Little cookie soldiers marching through the night
With their gumdrop buttons and their frosting smiles
The gingerbread rebellion’s going wild!

Bridge

The baker woke up to a big surprise
Cookies everywhere before her eyes
She laughed and said “Well I declare
A gingerbread party! I’ll join you there!”

Outro

Now every Christmas Eve when the clock strikes twelve
The gingerbread men come alive from the shelves
So if you hear tiny footsteps in the night
It’s just the cookie rebellion, and that’s alright!”

Snowlight Christmas

Verse 1

Snowflakes on the window, drifting soft and bright,
Candles in the doorway, glowing through the night.
Laughter in the kitchen, cocoa warm and sweet—
Christmas time is here again, bringing joy we greet.

Chorus

Oh, snowlight Christmas, shining in our hearts,
Every little moment feels like where the magic starts.
Hold this time together, never let it go—
Merry, merry Christmas in the soft December snow.

Verse 2

Footsteps on the sidewalk, friends are dropping by,
Warm gloves, wool scarves, watching stars up in the sky.
Stories by the fire, songs we love to sing—
Christmas makes the world feel full of hope and gentle things.

Chorus

Oh, snowlight Christmas, shining in our hearts,
Every little moment feels like where the magic starts.
Hold this time together, never let it go—
Merry, merry Christmas in the soft December snow.
